\section{Import from LaTeX}

This is a small LaTeX source. Running it through \texttt{pandoc -t json} and
then \texttt{pandocToCarve} yields the \texttt{paper.crv} next to this file.

\subsection{Emphasis and lists}

Pandoc maps \emph{emphasis} and \textbf{strong} to Carve's \texttt{/.../} and
\texttt{*...*} delimiters.

\begin{itemize}
  \item First bullet
  \item Second bullet
\end{itemize}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Ordered one
  \item Ordered two
\end{enumerate}

An inline formula $E = mc^2$ becomes Carve inline math.
